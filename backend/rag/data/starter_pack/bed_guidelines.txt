# BED MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES FOR AETHER-MED

## 1. Critical Cardiac Emergency
- **Placement Recommendations**: Immediate allocation to Cardiac ICU (CCU) or General ICU if CCU is full.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Continuous telemetric and hemodynamic monitoring.

## 2. Severe Allergy Conflict
- **Placement Recommendations**: Step-down unit or Medical ICU depending on the severity of anaphylaxis.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Continuous observation for biphasic allergic reactions.

## 3. ICU Candidate
- **Placement Recommendations**: ICU preferred. If ICU is at capacity, divert to Step-down unit with 1:1 nursing.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Full intensive care monitoring capabilities required at the bed station.

## 4. Respiratory Failure
- **Placement Recommendations**: ICU preferred.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Oxygen monitoring required. Bed must have direct access to piped medical oxygen and ventilator hookups.

## 5. Stroke Risk
- **Placement Recommendations**: Neuro-ICU or specialized Stroke Unit.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Frequent neuro-checks, dysphagia screening before oral intake.

## 6. Severe Asthma Attack
- **Placement Recommendations**: Step-down unit or ICU if impending respiratory failure.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Continuous pulse oximetry, frequent peak flow measurements.

## 7. Oncology Patient
- **Placement Recommendations**: Dedicated Oncology Ward.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Positive pressure room (Reverse Isolation) if the patient is severely neutropenic.

## 8. General Ward Patient
- **Placement Recommendations**: Standard Medical/Surgical Ward.
- **Monitoring Requirements**: Routine vitals monitoring (e.g., Q4H or Q8H).
