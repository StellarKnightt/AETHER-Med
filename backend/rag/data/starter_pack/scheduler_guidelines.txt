# SCHEDULER GUIDELINES FOR AETHER-MED

## 1. Critical Cardiac Emergency
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Prioritize immediate assignment to an Interventional Cardiologist.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Prioritize critical care nurse availability for transport and Cath Lab preparation.
- **Workload Balancing**: Bypass standard queue; preempt non-urgent consults.

## 2. Severe Allergy Conflict
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Assign an Immunologist/Allergist for follow-up, but primary emergency physician handles acute stabilization.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Ensure rapid response team nurse is available.
- **Workload Balancing**: High priority alert broadcast to pharmacy staff.

## 3. ICU Candidate
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Assign an Intensivist or Critical Care Specialist immediately.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Assign 1:1 or 1:2 nursing ratio based on acuity.
- **Workload Balancing**: Reallocate standard ward nurses if ICU step-down requires temporary coverage.

## 4. Respiratory Failure
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Prioritize Pulmonologist or Intensivist.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Immediate dispatch of a Respiratory Therapist to the bedside.
- **Workload Balancing**: Ensure RT workload allows for emergent intubation standby.

## 5. Stroke Risk
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Code Stroke activation requires an on-call Neurologist.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Stroke-certified nurse must accompany the patient to CT.
- **Workload Balancing**: Clear CT scanner schedule immediately.

## 6. Severe Asthma Attack
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Assign to Emergency Physician; consult Pulmonologist if refractory.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Respiratory therapist required for continuous nebulizers.
- **Workload Balancing**: Standard emergency department nursing ratios apply unless intubated.

## 7. Oncology Patient
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Assign to admitting Oncologist. Consult Infectious Disease if febrile neutropenia is present.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Chemotherapy-certified nurse required for specific treatments.
- **Workload Balancing**: Ensure isolation protocols do not overwhelm nursing assignment.

## 8. General Ward Patient
- **Specialist Assignment Guidance**: Assign to Hospitalist or General Internal Medicine physician.
- **Emergency Staffing Priorities**: Standard ward nursing ratios (e.g., 1:4 or 1:5).
- **Workload Balancing**: Distribute admissions evenly among available hospitalists.
