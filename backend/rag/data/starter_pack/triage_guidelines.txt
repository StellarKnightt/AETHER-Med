# TRIAGE GUIDELINES FOR AETHER-MED

## 1. Critical Cardiac Emergency
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Chest pain, radiating arm pain, shortness of breath, low blood pressure, low oxygen saturation.
- **Severity Indicators**: STEMI, elevated troponin, diaphoresis, impending sense of doom.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Requires immediate cardiology intervention. Do not delay in ER.
- **Triage Recommendations**: Critical Priority (Priority 1). Immediate physician review required. ICU consideration.

## 2. Severe Allergy Conflict
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Anaphylaxis, swelling of lips/tongue, hives, rapid pulse.
- **Severity Indicators**: Airway compromise, hypotensive shock.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Immediate epinephrine administration.
- **Triage Recommendations**: High to Critical Priority. Continuous monitoring required.

## 3. ICU Candidate
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Multi-organ failure, severe trauma, post-cardiac arrest.
- **Severity Indicators**: Requires mechanical ventilation, vasopressors, or continuous renal replacement.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Immediate escalation to intensivist.
- **Triage Recommendations**: Critical Priority (Priority 1). Requires immediate intensive care unit placement.

## 4. Respiratory Failure
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Severe dyspnea, cyanosis, accessory muscle use, altered mental status.
- **Severity Indicators**: O2 saturation < 88% on room air, hypercapnia on ABG.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Prepare for possible intubation. Page respiratory therapy immediately.
- **Triage Recommendations**: Critical Priority (Priority 1). Continuous pulse oximetry monitoring required.

## 5. Stroke Risk
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Unilateral weakness, facial droop, slurred speech, sudden loss of vision.
- **Severity Indicators**: Time since symptom onset (last known well), NIHSS score > 4.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Activate stroke code. Immediate non-contrast head CT required.
- **Triage Recommendations**: Critical Priority (Priority 1). Time-sensitive neurological emergency.

## 6. Severe Asthma Attack
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Audible wheezing, inability to speak in full sentences, chest tightness.
- **Severity Indicators**: Silent chest, paradoxical breathing, bradycardia.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Continuous albuterol nebulization. Consider magnesium sulfate or heliox.
- **Triage Recommendations**: High Priority. Requires rapid pharmacological intervention.

## 7. Oncology Patient
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Febrile neutropenia, sudden severe pain, spinal cord compression symptoms.
- **Severity Indicators**: Fever > 100.4 F in neutropenic patient, hypercalcemia.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Immediate broad-spectrum antibiotics for fever. Page oncology consult.
- **Triage Recommendations**: High Priority due to immunosuppression. Reverse isolation recommended.

## 8. General Ward Patient
- **Symptom Interpretation**: Stable vitals, manageable pain, minor infections, or post-operative recovery (uncomplicated).
- **Severity Indicators**: None. Vitals within normal limits.
- **Escalation Guidance**: Standard ward protocols. Routine rounding.
- **Triage Recommendations**: Low to Medium Priority. Safe for general admission.
