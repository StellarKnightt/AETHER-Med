# PHARMA GUIDELINES FOR AETHER-MED

## 1. Critical Cardiac Emergency
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Rapid administration of Aspirin, Nitroglycerin (if BP allows), and Heparin.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Avoid NSAIDs (other than Aspirin). Caution with beta-blockers if patient is in cardiogenic shock.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Continuous ECG monitoring. Monitor blood pressure frequently during nitroglycerin administration.

## 2. Severe Allergy Conflict (e.g., Penicillin Allergy)
- **Allergy Conflict Examples**: Patient allergic to Penicillin.
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Avoid all penicillin-based treatments (Amoxicillin, Piperacillin). Cross-reactivity risk with cephalosporins.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Recommend alternatives such as Macrolides (Azithromycin) or Fluoroquinolones.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Notify physician immediately if accidental exposure occurs. Monitor for anaphylaxis.

## 3. ICU Candidate
- **Medication Safety Rules**: High-risk medications (vasopressors, sedatives) require central line administration and double-checks.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Propofol requires monitoring of lipid levels. Watch for propofol infusion syndrome.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Continuous hemodynamic monitoring.

## 4. Respiratory Failure
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Cautious use of sedatives/opioids which can further depress respiratory drive unless patient is intubated.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Avoid non-selective beta-blockers in concomitant reactive airway disease.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Continuous capnography and pulse oximetry.

## 5. Stroke Risk
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Strict adherence to tPA inclusion/exclusion criteria. Do not administer anticoagulants/antiplatelets for 24 hours post-tPA.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Ensure BP is < 185/110 before administering thrombolytics.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Frequent neurological checks.

## 6. Severe Asthma Attack
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Systemic corticosteroids should be administered early.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Avoid beta-blockers.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Monitor potassium levels if continuous albuterol is used.

## 7. Oncology Patient
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Chemotherapy agents require specific handling protocols. Avoid live vaccines.
- **Interaction Guidance**: High risk of drug interactions with targeted therapies. Check CYP450 interactions.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Monitor complete blood counts and renal/hepatic function.

## 8. General Ward Patient
- **Medication Safety Rules**: Standard medication reconciliation on admission.
- **Interaction Guidance**: Standard interaction checks for polypharmacy.
- **Monitoring Recommendations**: Routine vital sign monitoring per ward protocol.
